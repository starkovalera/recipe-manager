# Prototype 17 plan

## Task

Move the approved desktop Basics, Ingredients, validation, and guard evidence out of temporary `.superpowers` output into the permanent design workspace.

## Preserved decisions

- Desktop option A: two semantic Basics zones.
- Sticky section rail plus ordinary page scrolling.
- One global Recipe Edit draft and Save/Cancel pair.
- Content-based control widths.
- No ingredient notes.
- Hybrid validation and linked failed-Save summary.
- Centered blocking desktop guard.
- Import Info under Overflow.

## Explicit update

Desktop Cooking time and Servings now match mobile: empty or positive whole number. This does not change the more expressive Quantity syntax.

## Out of scope

- final visual language;
- Instructions, Cooking notes, Nutrition, Manage Media, and Organize behavior;
- exact maximum lengths and Unit dictionary.
